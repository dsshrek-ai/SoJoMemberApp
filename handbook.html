02

Home & Announcements

Home shows whatever welcome message the director has posted — a good first stop when you open the app.

Announcements is where day-to-day news lives. Anything the director marks Pinned always shows up first; everything else runs newest to oldest below it.

03

Schedule

Every rehearsal and event, each tagged with a type badge (Rehearsal, Performance, etc.) and its date and time.

  • Tap View Map next to a location to open it in Google Maps.
  • Parking and entrance notes, when the director's added them, appear right under the location.
04

Songs & Parts

The Songs page lists everything on this season's setlist. Each song's card shows its status, when it was last rehearsed, and — if the director added them — a rehearsal track or YouTube link you can open directly.

Opening a song's parts

  1. From Songs, tap the song's title or its Parts & Tracks button.
  2. Under Choose Your Part, find your voice part — Soprano 1, Alto 2, whatever's listed.
  3. Use the inline player to listen right there, or tap Download to save the actual audio file to your device — handy for practicing offline or in the car.
Play streams the track in your browser and doesn't use up phone storage. Download saves the real file, named after the part, so you can find it again later in your Downloads folder.

Director Notes

If the director has posted notes for a song — phrasing reminders, a marked-up score, whatever — they'll appear below the parts as their own downloadable PDF.

05

Documents

Forms, handouts, and other downloads the director has posted, grouped into categories like Sheet Music or Forms. Tap any title to open it.

06

Volunteer Signups

Open tasks list how many slots are filled out of how many are needed — first come, first served.

Since you're already signed in, this page pulls in your name and phone on its own — give it a moment after the page loads and you'll see them fill in under Your Email, no typing required.
  1. Find a task with open slots and tap Sign Up.
  2. Check the name and phone number it filled in — edit either if something's out of date.
  3. Tap Confirm. A task showing Full has no slots left.

If the auto-fill doesn't kick in (or you'd rather look yourself up manually), type your email under Your Email and tap Look Up — or just fill in your name and phone by hand.

07

Report an Absence

Same auto-fill as Volunteer — after a brief pause, your name, phone, and section/position fill in on their own from your login, so you're not retyping it every time.

  1. Double-check the auto-filled fields — they come from the roster and can be wrong or out of date.
  2. Pick the date you'll be out and your section/position, add a note if you'd like, then tap Submit.

Nothing filled in? Look yourself up by email under Your Email, or just fill in the form by hand.

Submitting notifies your section leader automatically — there's no separate email to send.

08

My Info

After a brief pause, this page pulls up your own details automatically from your login — name, section/position, phone, and your attendance history, color-coded by status. No lookup required, though the same Your Email box is there if you ever need it.

This page also surfaces your section leader's contact card — tap to Call, Text, or Email them directly.

Something on this page wrong — a misspelled name, an old phone number? This page is read-only by design. Reach out to your section leader (right there on the same page) to get it corrected.
09

Recognition

A running feed of shout-outs the director posts for members — birthdays, milestones, a job well done.

10

Sponsors & Giving

A Support the Choir button links out to the donation page, followed by a list of current sponsors and their tier.

11

Join Us

Know someone who'd love to sing with us? Tap Copy Link to grab the registration link and paste it into a text or email.

This page also lists current audition opportunities, with a Sign Up to Audition button when one's open.

12

Countdown

When the director's set one, this page counts down live — days, hours, minutes, seconds — to the next big performance.

13

Tips & FAQ

Do I need an account?
Yes — log into My Apps Hub and open the choir app from there. That login is what lets Volunteer, Report Absence, and My Info recognize you automatically.
Why does it look like it's asking for my email?
It isn't, usually — give it a moment after the page loads and it fills in on its own, using the email you signed into My Apps Hub with. The email box is only there as a manual fallback, for the rare case the automatic lookup doesn't fire.
I looked up the wrong person — now what?
Tap Not you? next to the lookup result to clear it and start over.
The app doesn't recognize my email.
You can still use every page by hand — type your name, phone, and section directly into the form instead of relying on the lookup.
Does Download actually save the file, or does it just play it?
It saves it — tapping Download on a part track hands your device the real audio file, the same as downloading any attachment.

Add It to Your Phone or Computer

Since you always get here through My Apps Hub, the icon you install should point at My Apps Hub itself — not this handbook or the choir app directly — so tapping it takes you straight to your login, with SoJo Choral Arts one tap away from there.

The address to use everywhere below: seniorfamily.org/my-apps-hub.html

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open seniorfamily.org/my-apps-hub.html in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up, along the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Give it a name if you'd like (e.g. "My Apps"), then tap Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open seniorfamily.org/my-apps-hub.html in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app, if that's what shows up instead).
  4. Tap Add to confirm.

Windows or Mac (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open seniorfamily.org/my-apps-hub.html in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the three-dot menu in the top-right corner.
  3. In Chrome, go to Cast, save, and share → Create shortcut. In Edge, go to Apps → Install this site as an app.
  4. If you see an Open as window checkbox, check it — that makes it open on its own instead of inside a browser tab. Click Create (or Install). The icon lands on your desktop; drag it onto your taskbar or Start menu to keep it handy.
Once you tap that icon, log into My Apps Hub as usual, then open SoJo Choral Arts Seasons Chorale from your tile list — that login is what carries into every page in this handbook.
